How to read it. Expected is each holding's own per-share dividend growth from 2022 to now (capped at ±10%; bond funds set to 0%). Actual is the last 12 months: for steady payers, today's dashboard rate vs the rate a year ago; for variable, index and semi-annual payers, trailing-12-month totals. Specials and supplementals are left out; capital-gains distributors are excluded.
Status: on track within 1 point of expected or better · behind more than 1 point short · cut paying less per share than a year ago (down for variable payers).
Effect is each holding's share of the portfolio's actual rate: the column adds up to the total. Portfolio expected is the “mix rate”: it moves when you buy or sell. Portfolio actual only moves when a holding raises or cuts.
Flags: variable payout changes every period · FX foreign holdings, swings with exchange rates · rate-driven bond fund, follows interest rates · specials excl. regular dividend only. Rates from public dividend histories, updated by Claude at refreshes (). Weights and steady-payer rates are live from your dashboard.
